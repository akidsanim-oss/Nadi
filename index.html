The ritual

Four steps. Zero rush.

A calm loop you can run with our 3D desk — heat, bloom, pour, pause.

Brew like you mean it

1

Heat with intention

Match water to the leaf — never a rolling boil for jade greens.

2

Wake the leaves

A short rinse opens aroma and softens tightly rolled oolongs.

3

Steep, don’t bully

Time the infusion; swirl once; listen for the quiet bloom.

4

Pour & share

Decant fully so the next steep stays bright, not bitter.
