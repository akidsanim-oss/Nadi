Student Living Redefined

Everything student living should be

At 21 Oaks, we believe college living should enhance your academic journey, not complicate it. Our thoughtfully designed spaces give you the freedom to focus, connect, and thrive.

Private space

Your space to reset and focus. Each bedroom is designed as a personal retreat—quiet, comfortable, and perfectly sized for studying, sleeping, and recharging between classes.

  • Full-sized beds and study desks
  • Private climate control
  • Ample closet and storage space

Shared spaces

Room to connect, relax, and live beyond your apartment. Our open common areas are designed for collaboration, entertainment, and building memories with roommates.

  • Spacious living room with modern furniture
  • Full kitchen with stainless steel appliances
  • In-unit washer and dryer
