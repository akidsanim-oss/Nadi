The Art of the Leaf

The 3-Step Mindful Ritual

Three disciplined moments transform whole leaves into golden tranquility.

01

Waking the Clay

Pre-warm the hand-turned Yixing vessel with boiling mountain spring water to awaken the clay’s memory and mineral breath.

02

Aromatic Unfurling

Introduce whole coiled leaves into the heated chamber. Inhale the dense aromatic vapors before the first drop touches them.

03

Timed Infusion

Pour water with low turbulence. Steep for precisely 45 seconds to extract shimmering liquor without extracting harsh tannins.
