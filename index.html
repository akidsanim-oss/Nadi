04 — About the space

A room that changes colour as we speak

This empathetic space continues the conversation around the documentary Mà Sài Gòn, where the film characters talk about their dreams, love, and relationships. Add to the experience by sharing your own stories in the form of a letter, directing it to a specific recipient, and expressing your current emotional state.

The Mà Sài Gòn space will transform with every new letter to mirror our collective emotional state through a spectrum of colours.
