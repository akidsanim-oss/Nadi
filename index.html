WHAT IS TEPACHE?

Tepache (teh-PAH-chay) is Mexico’s treasured prebiotic pineapple brew, poured cold on street corners from plastic barrels into iced bags. We brought it modern.

🍍

Organic Pineapples

Real whole pineapples fermented with turbinado sugar, canela (cinnamon), and fragrant botanicals.

🦠

Gut-Loving Prebiotics

Naturally fermented to feed healthy microbiota, gently aiding digestion with every fizzy sip.

⚡

Under 40 Calories

No artificial sweeteners, no stevia aftertaste. Just certified organic fruit puree and street wisdom.

🇲🇽

Mercado Craft

Honoring the street-side tepacheros who have sustained Mexican neighborhoods for generations.

From Pre-Columbian Times to Today

THE TIMELINE OF TEPACHE

A sacred fermentation journey spanning centuries.

700 BC

The Nahua & Mayan Roots

The name Tepache originates from the Nahuatl word tepiatl ("drink made with corn"). Indigenous cultures fermented sacred grain brews for ritual feasts.

1500s

Pineapple Revolution

Pineapple skin fermentation replaced corn as Caribbean fruits spread through central Mexico, seasoned with cinnamon bark, star anise, and piloncillo.

1950s

Golden Age of Street Mercados

Neighborhood Tepacherías become social cornerstones in Mexico City and Guadalajara, served with chili powder rims in tied plastic bags with straws.

2021

De La Calle Launches Nationwide

Rafa and Alex bottle authentic canned Tepache, launching across Whole Foods Market and natural grocers across the United States.
